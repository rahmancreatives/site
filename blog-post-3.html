Google Ads vs. Meta Ads: Which is Better for Your Brand?

By Rahman Creatives | April 5, 2026

When planning your digital ad spend in Dubai, the age-old debate persists: Should you invest in Google Ads or Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)? The answer isn't a simple one-size-fits-all; it depends on your goals, audience, and product type.

Google Ads (Search Intent)

Google Ads targets users who are actively searching for your solution. If a Dubai user searches "best video editing agency," they are already in a buying mood. Google captures these high-intent leads, resulting in historically higher conversion rates.

Meta Ads (Social Discovery)

Meta Ads interrupt the user's browsing experience. While the intent is lower, the targeting capabilities are unmatched. You can target specific demographics, interests, and behaviors in Dubai to build brand awareness and retarget past website visitors.

The winning strategy? A combination of both. At Rahman Creatives, we use Google Ads to capture immediate demand and Meta Ads to build the brand awareness that fuels that demand.
